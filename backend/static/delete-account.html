Delete Your Zilo Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to request deletion of your Zilo account and associated data.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently deleted from our servers.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your Zilo account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account Information: Your phone number, business name, and profile details
  • Customer Data: All imported contacts, customer profiles, and conversation history
  • Messages: All WhatsApp message records stored in the CRM
  • Business Data: Products, orders, bookings, sales records, and analytics
  • Subscription Information: Your subscription status and payment history

Data Retention Period

Once you submit a deletion request:

  • Your account will be deactivated immediately
  • All personal and business data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Some transaction records may be retained for up to 90 days for legal and accounting purposes only
ℹ️ Note: Messages sent via WhatsApp are also stored in WhatsApp's servers. Deleting your Zilo account does not delete messages from WhatsApp itself. You must separately delete conversations in the WhatsApp app if needed.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your account, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to SAM@CREVO.APP
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Zilo"
  3. Include your registered phone number in the email body
  4. We will confirm your identity and process the deletion within 48 hours
📧 Send Deletion Request

Alternative: Delete Account In-App

You can also delete your account directly from the Zilo app:

  1. Open the Zilo app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or data privacy, please contact us at SAM@CREVO.APP.
