## 🎯 Smart Follow-Up Intelligence

### 1. **24-Hour & 3-Day Magic Windows**
The system knows the **proven sales timing**:
- **24 hours after contact**: Highest urgency (+40 score)
- **3 days after contact**: Perfect follow-up timing (+30 score)
- Automatically prioritizes customers in these windows

### 2. **Intelligent Urgency Scoring (0-100)**
Every customer gets a smart urgency score based on:

| Factor | Score Impact | Why It Matters |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Never contacted | +40 | New opportunity |
| Customer messaged you | +20 | They're interested! |
| Asked a question | +15 | Ready to buy |
| VIP customer | +25 | High value |
| New customer | +20 | Critical first impression |
| Has pending follow-up | -50 | Already handled |

**Result**: Only the most important customers appear in your list

### 3. **Smart Daily Limits**
The system adapts to your business size:
- **Small business** (<100 customers): Top 10 daily suggestions
- **Medium business** (100-500): Top 20 daily suggestions  
- **Power user** (500+): Top 30 daily suggestions

**No overwhelm** - just actionable insights you can handle
